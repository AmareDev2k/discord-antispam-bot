## 1. Description

The Bot helps server moderators detect and respond to spam involving
`@everyone`, `@here`, and image attachments. Depending on the configured
thresholds, the Bot may:

- Apply a Discord timeout to a suspected spammer.
- Delete messages identified as spam.
- Send a moderation summary to a configured staff log channel.

The Bot is a moderation aid and does not guarantee that every spam message will
be detected or that every detection will be correct.
